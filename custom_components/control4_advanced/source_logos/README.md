# Source logo provenance

The Apple TV, HBO Max, Netflix, Plex, Sony, Spotify, TIDAL, Xiaomi, and
YouTube glyph paths are derived from Simple Icons commit
`dc7b10662a0a2cfdfc9305e2a2b06c023c30724a`. Simple Icons is CC0-1.0;
individual brand names and marks remain trademarks of their respective
owners.

The TuneIn logo is the unmodified official `primary.svg` published at
`https://cms.tunein.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/primary.svg`.

The generic Watch, Listen, WiiM, and Xplore TV tiles were created for this
integration and contain no copied raster artwork.
